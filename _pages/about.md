I am currently pursuing a PhD in Economics at Sciences Po (Paris), under the supervision of [Roberto Galbiati](https://sites.google.com/view/galbiatir1/) and [Stefan Pollinger](https://www.stefanpollinger.com/).

My primary research interest is in understanding and quantifying the impact of the distributional and economic channels on environmental policy preferences. I study the impact of the coal mining job losses and subsequent compensation policies shift perceptions on the trade-off between jobs and the environment in Appalachia (United States).

My other research interests include the design and evaluation of environmental policies, including the EU Emission Trading System and policies for the transition to a circular economy. I also work more broadly on questions of environmental and political economics, as well as inequality.

Previously, I worked as a [Young Associate](https://www.oecd.org/en/about/careers/young-associates.html) and later as Research Associate at the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD), where I contributed to a number of projects, such as the development of the national circular economy strategies of Hungary and Slovakia, chapters in the Global Plastics Outlook (2022), and review papers on green fiscal stimulus, among others.
